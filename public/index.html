How Disaster Mesh Works

No satellite subscription or mobile phone plan required. Completely autonomous peer-to-peer store-and-forward relay.

1. No Internet or SIM Card Needed

Your phone connects via Wi-Fi Direct, Bluetooth, or local battery-powered LoRa packet nodes (865 MHz) installed on panchayat rooftops and rescue boats.

2. Peer-to-Peer Store & Forward

Emergency packets 'hop' from phone to phone and node to node until reaching a rescue team, satellite terminal, or relief camp gateway.

3. Triage & Verified Rescues

NDRF, OSDMA, and volunteer boats view live priority queues with GPS coordinates, medical urgency, and family headcounts.

Offline Mesh Setup & Emergency Survival Handbook

Downloadable guides and step-by-step hardware instructions to turn low-cost $5 ESP32 or LoRa boards into off-grid disaster communications relays.

1. Meshtastic / LoRa 865 MHz Radio Setup

How to configure standard LilyGO T-Beam, Heltec ESP32, or RAK Wireless nodes for India's license-free 865-867 MHz ISM band.

  • Frequency: 865.200 MHz (India WPC approved)
  • Modulation: LoRa, Bandwidth 250 kHz, SF11
  • Antenna: 868 MHz tuned whip or ground-plane
  • Power: 18650 Li-ion battery + 5V USB solar panel
