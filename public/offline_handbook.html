2. Immediate Life-Saving Survival Protocols

💧 Drinking Water Purification (No Power)

  • Household Bleach / Chlorine Drops: If clear water, add 2 drops of unscented household bleach (5-6% sodium hypochlorite) per 1 liter of water. Shake well and let stand for 30 minutes before drinking.
  • Boiling: If fuel is available, bring water to a rolling boil for a minimum of 3 to 5 minutes.
  • Halazone / Chlorine Tablets: 1 tablet per 20 liters. Wait 30 minutes.
  • Never drink stagnant flood water directly — risk of cholera and leptospirosis.

🐍 Snakebite Emergency Protocol

  • DO NOT cut the bite wound or attempt to suck venom.
  • DO NOT apply ice, electricity, or tight arterial tourniquets.
  • DO immobilize the bitten limb using a splint or sling at heart level.
  • DO remove rings, watches, or tight clothing near the bite before swelling starts.
  • Transport calmly to the nearest District Hospital (DHH) for polyvalent antivenom.

🌀 Cyclone Wind & Surge Safety

  • Do not shelter under tin sheds, asbestos roofs, or near coconut trees. Move immediately to a reinforced concrete Multi-Purpose Cyclone Shelter (MPCS).
  • The Eye of the Cyclone: If violent winds suddenly stop and the sky clears, do not go outside. The calm center is passing; catastrophic hurricane-force winds will resume from the opposite direction within 20 to 45 minutes.
  • Turn off main electrical breaker and gas cylinders before flood waters enter the home.

3. Hardware Guide: Off-Grid Mesh Relays (LoRa 865 MHz)

Low-cost ($5 to $25) ESP32-based LoRa radios allow battery-operated peer-to-peer communication across 5 to 15 kilometers without any internet service.

India Frequency & Regulatory Specification (WPC Approved):

  • Operating Frequency: 865.200 MHz (License-Free ISM Band for India)
  • Modulation: LoRa, Bandwidth 250 kHz, Spreading Factor SF11, Coding Rate 4/8
  • Transmit Power: Up to 20 dBm (100 mW) — strictly compliant with WPC guidelines

Wiring an ESP32 to SX1262 / SX1276 LoRa Module:

ESP32 Pin       SX1262 LoRa Module
----------------------------------
GPIO 18  -----> SCK  (SPI Clock)
GPIO 19  -----> MISO (SPI Master In)
GPIO 23  -----> MOSI (SPI Master Out)
GPIO 5   -----> NSS  (Chip Select)
GPIO 26  -----> DIO1 (Interrupt)
GPIO 14  -----> RST  (Reset)
3.3V     -----> VCC  (Use clean 3.3V power, NOT 5V!)
GND      -----> GND

Power with an 18650 3.7V rechargeable Li-ion battery and 5V 5W portable solar panel mounted on a bamboo pole or rooftop.
